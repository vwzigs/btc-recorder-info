BTC Recorder

Record public Bitcoin market data and keep a copy in your own Google Drive.

What the recorder does

BTC Recorder collects public Coinbase Bitcoin spot prices and Kalshi Bitcoin prediction-market data for later research. It stores time-stamped observations, market information, and outcomes on the owner's server.

When the owner connects Google Drive, the recorder periodically copies compressed data archives and progress files to a dedicated backup folder in that account.

Why it requests Google Drive access

Drive access provides the optional backup feature. The app requests the drive.file permission to create and manage its backup files and folders. It works with files created by the app or explicitly made available to it.

The owner authorizes access directly with Google and controls the destination account. See the privacy policy for storage, retention, and access controls.

Operation and control

The owner runs the recorder on their server and can stop collection or backups at any time. Google Drive authorization can be revoked from the owner's Google Account connections settings.

These public pages describe the utility. Research archives are stored on the owner's server and in the owner's Drive backup folder.
