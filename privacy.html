BTC Recorder

Privacy policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

BTC Recorder is a personal research utility maintained by vwzigs. This policy describes its optional Google Drive backup feature and these informational pages.

Information the recorder handles

The recorder collects public Bitcoin spot-price observations and prediction-market data, including market information, order books, and outcomes. It creates compressed archives, integrity information, market summaries, and recording-progress files.

The owner chooses the Google account used for backups. Google provides authorization tokens so the server can upload backups and refresh access without repeated sign-in.

Google Drive permissions and use

The app requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. This permits access to files and folders created by the app or explicitly authorized for it. The backup feature uses that access to create, list, and update its backup folder and files.

Google account access is used to provide and maintain the owner's chosen backups. The app does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Backup data is not shared by the app with other users.

Storage and service providers

Research data is stored on the owner's server hosted by Oracle Cloud and copied to the owner's Google Drive account when backups are enabled. Authorization tokens are stored in the server's rclone configuration for backup operations. Credentials are excluded from the recorder's backup file selection.

Oracle provides server hosting and Google provides account authorization and Drive storage. Their services process data as needed to perform those functions under their own terms and privacy policies.

Retention, deletion, and revocation

The owner controls retention of local data and Drive backups. Copies remain in their respective storage locations until the owner removes them, subject to the storage provider's retention and trash policies.

The owner can stop the recorder or disable backups on the server. To revoke Google access, open Google Account connections and remove the app's access. Revocation stops future authorized access; it does not automatically erase existing archives.

To remove stored data, delete the relevant archives on the server and the backup folder in Google Drive, and manage Drive trash as needed.

This informational website

These pages contain no analytics scripts, embedded trackers, or sign-in forms. They are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors' IP addresses for security purposes under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be published here with an updated effective date. For project and privacy questions, contact the owner through the project repository.
