Personal research utility

BTC Recorder is operated by its owner to collect public Bitcoin market observations and preserve research archives. These pages explain the utility and its optional Google Drive backup feature.

Account access and operation

Use the backup feature with a Google account and storage that you are authorized to use. The account owner chooses whether to authorize access and can revoke it through their Google Account connections settings.

The owner controls the server, recording settings, backup destination, storage capacity, and retention of copies. They can stop the utility and remove their stored files at any time.

Privacy and changes

The privacy policy explains the data handled by the recorder, Google Drive permissions, storage, and deletion controls. Updates to these terms will be posted here with an updated effective date.
